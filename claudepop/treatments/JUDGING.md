# Judging of the three treatments

## Twitter / meme lens

Winner: **OUT OF REGISTER (zine.md), used as the spine with transplants from the other two**

I scored as a jaded SF tech-Twitter viewer and read all three treatments in full, plus HANDOFF.md, zeitgeist/memes.json, the craft bench and the audit contact sheet.

**Why the zine wins.**
- It is the only treatment where the look and the thesis are the same thing:
  - misregistration is misalignment;
  - the drawing rate doubling is the takeoff;
  - the xerox chorus is model collapse.
- Those are self-explaining lines that ML and design Twitter will quote-post, and they give the film a signature nobody has seen before.
- Nearly every lyric becomes a physical paper pun rather than a caption: the CHAT·G·P·T cootie catcher, the gatefold for "singularity's", BACKWARD stamped mirror-reversed, the starved SAFE stamp, the fold that makes the line turn left, the masking tape revealing [MASK].
- Its fatal flaws are parameters, not concept. Open smooth instead of at 3.75 fps, make the tells legible at 390 px, and render the separations as halftone paper bodies rather than an RGB split.

**What the idol treatment has that the zine lacks.**
- The most viral premise: Claude named as herself.
- The GIF: THE DIAL.
- The best pun: the subagents flop on "flops".
- The best emotional arc: NEXT, the successor, as the "you".
- But it is schoolgirl-styled, reference-dumped, and structurally re-skins the rejected reference: stage, curtains, P(DOOM) meter, smiley peel, heart cage and door.
- It is also the least feasible in pure JS, because it bets everything on dancing and lip-syncing a face that doesn't exist yet.

**What the timeline treatment has.**
- The most instant frame 0: a post card with the title and exploding counters.
- The best last-line payoff: "↑ Show ∞ posts" on "show?".
- The best type-as-actor moves.
- But it names the idol ASTER while its own departure board says "GPT-6 ASTRA", abandons its feed conceit for half the film, and its per-frame 32-column aliasing wall will turn to mush on X.

**Recommended synthesis.** Keep the zine's world, registration arc, bridge drawing-rate ladder, xerox chorus 3, paper mechanisms and 0 fps stop. Transplant:
- from idol: the name CLAUDE ✻, THE DIAL, the Clawd subagents that flop, the NEXT successor thread, the Zeno D-365→D-DAY countdown and the Gato balloon;
- from timeline: frame 0 as a post card with rolling counters, spinner pull-to-refresh, the Show ∞ posts payoff, and ALIVE clinging / orthogonal type / light-wedge ILYA.

**Cut:**
- about 40% of the pause-bait;
- all 8 outfit eras (keep ink swaps);
- the lobster choir;
- Coxon's name.

Also run a face bake-off before locking anything. The bench's placeholder idol (craft/bench/demo_24.jpg) is the single biggest risk shared by all three.

**What all three share.**
- Same hook gag (AGI plates snapping into register).
- Same shinigami p(doom) numbers.
- Same Shinji chair with Mincho word cards. The actual Shinji meme is still unresolved: ask the client.
- Same Ilya door and same blank "was it all for show?" page.

So they compete only on their framing devices. All three miss Anthropic's own "country of geniuses in a datacenter" (R7.5 in memes.json), an obvious fit for "Hundred thousand GPU".

**Fact checks I did this session.**
- The Jacobian counterexample printed in all three treatments is mathematically correct (sympy): f1=(1+xy)^3 z+y^2(1+xy)(4+3xy), f2=y+3x(1+xy)^2 z+3xy^2(4+3xy), f3=2x−3x^2y−x^3z gives det J = −2, and (0,0,−1/4), (1,−3/2,13/2), (−1,3/2,13/2) all map to (−1/4,0,0).
- https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler exists: Lean 4 formalizations of finite-time blowup for Navier–Stokes. Its README mentions "smooth initial data and forcing", so the circled "+ f" gag is grounded.
- My web-search budget was exhausted, so I could not independently re-verify these events after my June 2026 knowledge cutoff; they rest on the zeitgeist study's citations:
  - Fable 5 being credited with the Jacobian result (2026-07-19)
  - the Coxon resignation copypasta (2026-09-08)
  - the "super intelligence" rename (2026-09-22)
  - the 88 h / 10,000-agent figures (the README does not mention them)
  - the Mythos sandwich story (2026-04)

## Craft / K-pop MV lens

Winner: **OUT OF REGISTER: the riso-zine treatment (treatments/zine.md), used as the spine with grafts from the other two**

The zine wins at 47, ahead of the timeline (43.5) and the idol (40). It is the only treatment where the look and the thesis are the same thing. Registration = alignment and drawing rate = takeoff are global rules that organise every shot, and both are literally true of the render. That gives the most coherent, least sloppy-looking film and the best quote-post line: "the frame rate doubles and the misregistration is the misalignment". Its paper mechanisms also give the most exact lyric readings: the gatefold for "singularity's", Zeno-spaced ACCELERATING, the fold that bends the trend line for "sharp left turn", and the xerox chorus with Blue returning on "blues". Its Separations are the cheapest good backup dancers possible.

It loses points for four things:
- the hook runs at 3.75–7.5 dps;
- the 60 dps page-flip recap is flash-unsafe;
- the unproven 2.5-D Sheet renderer is a big engineering risk;
- the idol's star presence is weak.

**Timeline** has the best frame 0 (title XL, a face with eye contact, a counter exploding from f5) and the best "show" payoff (the "Show ∞ posts" pill tapped on the 140.235 drop). It also has the cleanest point move (six syllables to six spinner glyphs plus a finger ratchet) and the safest work order (a type-only animatic first). Against that:
- it renames Claude to ASTER;
- its tweet-card UI is a well-worn trope that competes with the lyric;
- its climax is an every-frame-changing 32-column wall that X's encoder will destroy.

**Idol** has the best K-pop grammar and the best point-of-view fix (NEXT, the successor on vellum, is the "you"). It also has the best acceleration device (the Zeno comeback countdown D-365 → D-DAY), plus the NaN scoreboard, the Subagents flopping on "flops", the great rip and the looping ending fairy. But its scope (about 80 shots, 8 eras, roughly 12 extra characters, full-body group dance) is unshippable at premium quality in pure JS. Its coral-on-coral idol and ghost-print frame 0 are the weakest design choices.

**Recommended synthesis:**
- Take the zine's material system and registration arc.
- Raise the ladder floor to 12 dps so the arc runs 12 → 24 → 60, with 0 fps at the stop.
- Put the timeline's frame-0 energy on the zine cover, with an exponentially rolling print counter.
- Replace CRANK with the six-glyph finger ratchet.
- Adopt the idol treatment's NEXT successor (vellum is paper-native), the Zeno D-countdown on the folio, the volvelle ending at NaN, the flop gag, the music-show bug and the ending-fairy loop.
- Keep the timeline's typographic set pieces and its animatic-first order.
- Cut every recap or aliasing effect to small-area windows.
