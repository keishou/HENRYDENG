**Angle: OMEGA POINT (matter, time, convergence).** Written 2026-09-28 for the new direction: a stream-of-consciousness,
philosophical, high-aesthetic music short film, with the user as the protagonist.

## 0. The idea in one paragraph

Every frame of this film is a one-point perspective drawing, and a small orange point sits at its vanishing point. A man
rebuilt by a machine from one photograph walks through a world whose lines all lead there. Over 156.65 s the film first
lets him breathe, then dissolves him into points, then carries him up the curve of complexity (body, then noosphere, then
Ω). At the loudest moment of the song, everything in the film streams into that point: the rain, the crowd, the text, and
him. Then a breath fogs the glass over his photograph, and the fog shrinks to a point and goes out. That is the film, and
everything below serves it.

## 1. Title, logline, thesis, voice

**Title.** *THE WITHIN · 万物之内.* The title comes from "The Within of Things" (*Le dedans des choses*), chapter 2 of Book
One of Teilhard de Chardin's *Le Phénomène humain*. He wrote the book in Peking in 1938-40, and it was published after
his death in 1955.

**Logline.** A machine rebuilds a man from one photograph, lets him breathe, dissolves him and carries him up the curve
of complexity into the Omega Point. When everything has converged, what remains is the one thing the machine never
measured.

**Thesis.**
1. Teilhard, a Jesuit palaeontologist who worked in China for about twenty years, held that all matter has a *without*
   that science measures and a *within* that it cannot. As matter grows more complex (atom, cell, brain, network), the
   within rises, until "tout ce qui monte converge" (everything that rises must converge) on one final point, Omega.
2. The singularity is the same faith redrawn as a compute curve, and in a machine's hands convergence means compression:
   to enter the point, a thing must first be reduced to what can be measured.
3. So the film carries one man, measured once and only from the front, up that curve into the point. It ends on what did
   not converge: a breath on glass, the within, which no reconstruction contains.

**The hidden spine (never stated on screen).**
- Teilhard wrote the *Phénomène* in Peking, and his fieldwork was tied to the Zhoukoudian fossils.
- In December 1941 the original Peking Man bones (at least 40 individuals) were packed for shipment to New York and
  vanished. Only Weidenreich's plaster casts and drawings survive.
- The best-known face of Peking Man is a 1937 bust ("Nellie") that the sculptor Lucile Swan modelled on Weidenreich's
  reconstructed skull.
- So the most famous early human in China is a reconstruction from fragments whose originals are lost, and our
  protagonist is a reconstruction from one photograph.

Two dated lines of the ticker carry this (section 4.9) for whoever looks.

**How the meme lyrics are transformed.** Each lyric is read at its own scale on Teilhard's ladder and gets one image at
that scale. The humour survives only as deadpan interface text and editing: never a meme picture, never a gag performed
by the actor.

| Lyric family | Reading on the ascent | Image register | Deadpan layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| The names prayed to (ChatGPT, Sydney, Gato) | the noosphere's earlier faces, invoked like saints. In Teilhard's *Mass on the World* (Ordos Desert, 1923), with no bread or wine, he offered the whole earth as the host; "please don't eat me alive" is the same offering seen from the bread's side | three prayers into one overhead light (B07 flesh, B18 points, B31a memory) | none: the prayers are played straight |
| Mechanisms (training loss, circuits, forward/backward, Chinchilla, masked pre-training, recursive self-upgrade) | complexification: matter learning | nature doing the maths: the horizon steps down like a loss curve, wrist veins, a palindrome walk, overprinted type, `[MASK]` blocks, Droste | the text finishes before the singer (B04) |

**Who is singing.** The voice is the noosphere, Teilhard's word for the thinking layer that humanity wraps around the
Earth. A language model is that layer compressed, and this voice is itself generated: osmarks (fetched 2026-09-28) lists
Udio originals and a Suno "Claude-Pop" variant. It belongs to nobody and is made of everybody. It sings "I" because it is
made of first persons. It says "you" to him because he is the one it is taking in, and that inversion ("I see sparks of
AGI in *your* eyes") casts the reconstructed man as the next mind. The edit rules that follow from this:
1. **Verses:** the voice speaks to him. He is frontal: the measured face in Act I, the frontal point-figure after the
   pivot.
2. **Choruses:** the voice speaks as itself. He is small and silhouetted in the RUNWAY master, never in close-up. In
   chorus 4 he turns his back and walks into the point.
3. **Pre-choruses:** the three names are prayers, and he lifts his face into one overhead light.
4. **At Ω:** the song runs out of words (the outro vocal 141.1-152.95 is wordless, per `song.json`), so the film runs out
   of text. Convergence ends language.
5. **Colour:** orange marks the active sung word because it is the voice's colour, never his. He never sings, and there
   is no lip-sync anywhere: his mouth is closed or neutral in every plate.

## 2. The opening

**0-3 s (what a muted viewer sees and reads).**
- **0.000-0.236.** True black (6 frames).
- **0.236 (the pad and the first beat).** One orange point appears at frame centre, with a tiny mono chip beside it: `1`.
- **Every 16th note (0.1136 s), the points double.** The chip races `2 · 4 · 8 … 1,024 … 16,384 · 32,768` and reaches
  `65,536` exactly on the bar-2 downbeat (2.054 s: 16 doublings in one bar).
  - Up to about 0.9 s the points scatter outward from the centre and read as stars.
  - By about 1.3 s they form filaments.
  - By about 1.8 s a shape appears.
  - On the downbeat it is his face: the measured front surface only, frontal and silent. The chip now reads
    `POINTS 65,536 · FROM 1 PHOTOGRAPH`.
  - The first orange point has settled into his eye as the catchlight.
- **1.145.** The 2.39:1 letterbox and hairline corner marks fade in.
- **2.045 ("I").** A giant serif **I** lands in the left third. **see** follows at 2.50 and **sparks** at 2.852, when the
  points of both irises flare for 4 frames.
- **At 3.0 s** the frame holds three things: a face made of 65,536 points with flaring eyes; the words *I / see / sparks*
  in type 25-30 % of the image height; and the premise in one mono line.

**3-10 s.**
- **3.435-3.9.** *of AGI* arrives letter by letter on the sung parts: A at 3.635, then G, then I.
- **4.77-5.23.** On *in your eyes* the camera pushes into his right-hand eye.
- **5.690 (downbeat).** The camera falls through the catchlight and comes out of a **real, living eye** with the same
  orange catchlight: the first flesh (B03). The THOUGHT *your circuits make me nervous,* sits beside it, with a hairline
  from the pupil to a chip `IRIS · MEASURED`.
- **7.508.** THE ROOM: he sits still at a table, facing us, rain on the window behind him. The text *that's no surprise*
  **finishes typing about 200 ms before the singer reaches "surprise"** (8.64).
- **9.327.** THE PLAIN: a salt flat at dusk, the horizon at mid-frame, and him tiny, seen from behind. At 10.70 the
  horizon will step down.

**Why a muted viewer stays.**
1. **A number doubling on screen is the most readable form of acceleration.** It creates suspense within half a second,
   before any picture exists.
2. **A face assembling out of a starfield forces pattern completion.** The eye cannot leave until it resolves, and it
   resolves at 2.05 s.
3. **The type is readable at phone size and carries the song.** The first word is one letter a third of the frame high,
   and the cards build word by word on the singing.
4. **The reveal comes at 5.69 s.** A match cut from data to a living eye says *this is a real person*, and the mono line
   has already said he was made from one photograph.
5. **The machine runs ahead of the voice at 8.4 s.** The text knows the lyric before it is sung, which is uncanny.

This hook needs no generated footage before 5.69 s (it is pure 3D and type), so it survives any fal failure.

## 3. The protagonist: arc, body, reconstruction, face angles

### 3.1 Inner arc

| Act | Span (s) | HUD chapter | His body | What he does | Inner state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha | 0-2.05 | `00 · MATTER` | 65,536 points assembling | nothing: he is not yet anyone | - |
| I | 2.05-49.33 | `01 · BIOSPHERE` | flesh (generated plates), measured face, visible breath | looks, sits, stands on the plain, holds out his photograph, prays, walks the runway toward us | embodied, watched, nervous; afraid of being eaten (B07) |
| Pivot | 49.33-52.96 | changes on 52.963 | flesh re-sorting into points, inferred parts first | looks at his raised hand | curiosity, not horror: the atoms rearrange and the shape stays |
| II | 52.96-109.07 | `02 · NOOSPHERE` | a point-figure; flesh only in reflections and memory | prays as points and lets himself go; loops; meets his double; disperses into the room; sits in the chair | dispersal and loneliness; the false convergence (paperclips); the fork (orthogonality) |
| III | 109.07-140.24 | `03 · CONVERGENCE` | a point-figure, back to us | disobeys (turns away), walks through the fences, reaches the glass, sees himself in the light, and turns to us in the frozen stop | choice: the within as a direction, Omega rather than paperclips |
| Ω | 140.24-152.96 | `Ω` | all points | walks into the point | - |
| Return | 152.96-156.65 | `01 · BIOSPHERE` | only his photograph | a breath fogs the glass | what is left over |

The choice at the heart of Act III is the orthogonality thesis made personal. The same convergence can end in a mind
(Teilhard's Omega) or in one dead form (Bostrom's paperclips), and intelligence alone does not decide which (B35). He
turns his back on the audience (B38) and walks toward the light, not the room full of clips. The film never says what
Omega is: at B43 we see him see it, and we do not see it.

### 3.2 What we see of him, and what we never see

- **We see:**
  - his frontal face in flesh: Act I plates, the memory stills (B31a-d) and one reflection (B43);
  - the one photograph, the only unprocessed image of him in the film (B06, C08, C10, B46);
  - his hands in macro;
  - his body as a backlit silhouette in wides;
  - the point-figure from any angle after the pivot.
- **We never see:**
  - a photoreal profile or back of his head;
  - his face at an extreme angle;
  - lip-sync;
  - the avatar's textured CG face in close-up;
  - his face "explained" by text.
- **The point cloud is honest by construction.** Points on the measured front of the face are dense and carry the
  photo's tone. Points on inferred surfaces (sides and back of the head, and the whole MakeHuman body) are sparser, flat
  grey `#767672`, and boil at 12 fps (section 4.5). Any angle of the point-figure is therefore allowed, because it shows
  what was seen and what was guessed.

### 3.3 How "reconstructed from one photograph" is used

The film uses the fact openly but explains it only through interface labels (`FROM 1 PHOTOGRAPH`, `MEASURED`,
`INFERRED`). It lands in four beats:
1. **B01:** he is assembled from points before he is ever flesh.
2. **B17:** at the pivot, the inferred parts dissolve first and the measured eyes last. The data's edges are the first
   to go.
3. **C07:** one beat of the turntable shows the back of his head in flat grey inferred material. It is the film's single
   deliberate look at the unseen side, on "pre-training", the masked-token idea by which that back was made.
4. **B46:** the source photograph, flat and still. After everything, the measured front is all there ever was, and the
   breath on the glass is the within that it never held.

The generated plates are inference too, and the texture admits it. Their halftone pitch is fine only inside the frontal
face region (a tracked landmark mask) and coarser on hair, body and background (section 4.5).

### 3.4 Face-angle rules (binding)

1. **Flesh close-ups and mediums:** yaw within ±30° of frontal (aim for ±15°). Pitch runs from -15° to +25°, since the
   prayers lift the face while staying frontal.
2. **Profiles (30-90°):** only as rim-lit silhouettes with the face black, or as the point-figure with its sparse grey
   inferred side (B19b).
3. **Back of the head:** silhouette, or the point-figure's grey back. Flat inferred grey is used exactly once, on purpose
   (C07).
4. **The avatar's textured face** never appears larger than 8 % of frame height. Closer than that, the avatar is shown
   only as points, grey clay or silhouette. The check renders (`out/avatar/check/sheet_pose.jpg`) show why: the body
   reads, but the hair and skin read as CG.
5. **The photo-textured bust** (front = the photo) is the 3D stand-in for any frontal close-up within ±20°, and it is
   the fallback for every face plate.
6. **Expression:** neutral, mouth closed. No smiles, no acting toward the lyric.

### 3.5 The 3D body's four jobs

1. **Animatic stand-in** for every flesh shot. It locks timing, framing and eyelines before any credit is spent.
2. **Source for previs-to-plate.** Route B (GENMEDIA section 4) restyles our render into photoreal video with the
   camera and timing kept: B04, B09, B11 and B16.
3. **Source of the point-figure.** The skinned MakeHuman surface is sampled into points every frame, and the bust
   point cloud (about 450k points) replaces the head, measured front dense and inferred rest sparse.
4. **Silhouettes** in the runway and plain, and as the fallback for every generated wide.

**Wardrobe anchor.** He wears the black henley with one small gold button at the collar, dark trousers and black shoes,
matching the avatar build. The button is the one warm object on him: the point he carries. On the point-figure it is a
single orange point at the collar, and it is the last point of him to merge into Ω (B45).

**Mocap clips used** (curated CMU set, `odyssey/body/curate_motion.py`):
- slow straight walk, time-warped to half-time, then to every beat (B09, B16, B38-B42, B45);
- stop and look up (B07, B18, B26);
- reach, meaning raise hand or wipe (B17, B28);
- sit on a seat prop (B04, B11, B25, B32b), and face in hands (B33);
- the 360° step-turn, cut to 180° (B38);
- quiet stand and breathe (B05, B13, B43).

Not used: run, kneel, floor sit, dance. The body stays calm, and the world does the moving.

## 4. Style

### 4.1 Look: "silver nocturne, one point"

This fuses two of the looks under test and drops the third:
- **From the fashion film:** anabology's cinematography (long lenses, straight-on symmetrical frames, wet reflective
  floors, backlight through rain and fog) and its editorial HUD.
- **From the point-cloud reconstruction look:** the point states, which are **not a separate VFX look**. Points are
  rendered in the same silver on the same near-black, under the same grade and halftone, so flesh and data share one
  photographic stock.
- **Dropped:** filmic monochrome chiaroscuro survives only as the lighting rule (one motivated key). On its own it would
  drift toward the odyssey film.

Most of the film is black, silver and orange. Saturation on plates is at most 0.12, blacks are lifted to about 0.03, and
the one saturated colour is the orange of the point and the voice.

### 4.3 Light

**The key light is always the point.**
- On the runway the key is the lamp at the vanishing point, so he is backlit when he faces us, with a low front
  footlight for readability.
- In the Room it is the window.
- In the prayers it is one overhead source (the point above).
- In the return it is the light table beneath the photograph.

No fill without a motivating source, no lens flares, no coloured gels. Rain and fog exist to make the light visible.

### 4.5 Texture: the halftone is sampling resolution

- **A round-dot AM halftone at 45°,** multiplied over every frame. It deliberately differs from anabology's square
  4.2 px LCD-like screen.
- **The pitch encodes knowledge.** It is 3 px on measured surfaces (the frontal face region, or the dense front points)
  and 6-12 px on inferred surfaces and backgrounds, driven by a per-pixel "measured" mask.
- **At Ω the pitch falls to 0:** the dots vanish into continuous white, because at the point nothing is inferred.
- **Grain:** fine monochrome film grain (σ ≈ 0.015) on flesh plates only. No scanlines, no RGB split, no glitch.

### 4.6 Frame rate and boil

- **24 fps master.** A beat is 10.909 frames, so every cut is rounded to the nearest frame of the beat grid. The chorus-4
  montage therefore runs mostly 11-frame shots with an occasional 10-frame one. All cut frames are listed in section 6.
- **Boil:** measured points hold still between frames, while inferred points re-jitter at 12 fps (on 2s). The guess
  shimmers and the measurement does not.
- **Memory stills** are held frames with 6-frame dissolves.

### 4.9 HUD: the machine's telemetry

**Layout.**
- Hairline corner marks sit 24 px in, and text margins are 72 px.
- Top left holds the chapter label with an orange point as its bullet: `• 01 · BIOSPHERE`.
- Top right holds the one-way counter, **`CLOCK ×1`**.
- A 12 px chronological **ticker** runs along the bottom edge (y ≈ 1058, hairline above).
- Tracking chips (inverted NIGHT on SILVER) appear only where a shot needs them.
- **At most two HUD elements plus one type block per frame.**

**The CLOCK** is his subjective rate relative to wall time: how much faster the reconstructed mind now runs. It is a
designed diegetic value, not a measurement.

| Span or event | CLOCK |
|---|---|
| 0-47.62 | ×1 |
| creeps from "accelerating" | ×2 by 49.52 |
| the pivot | ×10 |
| chorus 2 | ×10² |
| verse 3 | ×10⁴ |
| hidden in the breakdown | ×10⁶ |
| one step per "just" | ×10⁸-10¹¹ |
| bridge | ×10¹⁴ by 125.69 |
| chorus 4 | ×10¹⁸ by 131.995 |
| 138.417 | the counter freezes with the world |
| 140.236 | ×∞ for 4 frames, then `Ω`, then gone |
| 152.963 | `CLOCK ×1` |

**The world slows as the clock rises.** A mind that runs faster sees the world slow down, so the composited rain falls
at speed = CLOCK^(-1/6):

| CLOCK | Rain speed |
|---|---|
| ×10 | 68 % |
| ×10² | 46 % |
| ×10⁴ | 21 % |
| ×10⁶ | 10 % |
| ×10¹² | 1 % |
| the stop | exactly 0 |

This is the film's time-compression logic in one line. Outside, history accelerates (the ticker). Inside, the present
dilates until it stops (the rain). They meet at the stop, where the world hangs frozen, and Ω releases it.

**The ticker** is a chronology that runs from the Big Bang to today across the film, at a log-compressed pace:
- **Speed:** 120 px/s in Act I, 240 in Act II, 480 in Act III and 960 in chorus 4. At that speed it is an unreadable
  blur, which is the point.
- **Arrival:** it reaches `2026-09-28 · TODAY` at 131.995 (C14 prints it at frame centre).
- **Absence:** there is no ticker in the breakdown or in Ω, because there is nothing after today.
- **Format:** every item is dated.

### 4.12 Kill list (never)

- Points that swirl, orbit decoratively or blow away in wind. Points move in only three ways: they re-sort in rows, they
  travel in straight lines toward the point, or they hang.
- Glowing blue brains, circuit boards or holograms.
- Radial hyperspace streaks. The Ω corridor is rectilinear runway geometry stretched by slit-scan, not a starfield.
- Colour Earth-from-space hero shots. B24's Earth is night lights as points, in silver.
- Lens flares, chromatic aberration, RGB glitch.
- Split-flap boards and month countdowns (anabology's signatures).
- The bone-to-satellite match cut.
- Meme images.
- Lip-sync.
- Any smile or reaction acted to a lyric.

## 5. Recurring motifs

1. **THE POINT (Ω, orange).** It returns at every scale, and it is always the same point:
   - the origin (B01);
   - the catchlight (B02-B03);
   - the orange irises (B14);
   - the lamp at the end of every runway;
   - the match flame (B10) and the fuse spark (B34);
   - the moon, whose light is only reflected (B22);
   - the point named `Ω · COMING SOON` (B23);
   - the pole star (B26, C03);
   - the gold button at his collar;
   - the centre of all 14 chorus-4 cuts;
   - the light behind the glass (B43);
   - the whole frame at Ω (B45);
   - the last patch of fog over his eye, which goes out on the bass cut (B46).
2. **BREATH (the within).** Breath is the only visible sign that a thing has an inside warmer than the world.
   - Vapour rises into the prayer light (B07).
   - The breath becomes a cloud of points rising into the light (B18-B19), the "let me free" of the Sydney prayer.
   - A breath hangs frozen as points before his mouth in the stop (B44).
   - Fog blooms on the glass over the photograph and evaporates to a point (B46).
   - A 2-bar breath cycle (exhale on odd downbeats, about 16.5 breaths per minute) is shared by the flesh plates' vapour
     layer, the point-face's scale and the Room.
3. **THE BRANCH that becomes a CONE.** One branching generator is used at every scale, first growing outward, then
   running in reverse.
   - Outward: lightning (B05, B15), wrist veins (B06), mycelium (B12), the cosmic web (B24), the mycelium covering the
     Room (B30b).
   - Then inward: the delta flowing into one trunk (B37), light paths merging (C02), Loom warp threads pulled to the
     centre (C04), the mycelium retracting into its bag (C05), and at last every line of the film into Ω (B45).
   - Teilhard's tree of life, turned into his cone of convergence.
4. **THE ONE PHOTOGRAPH (the source).**
   - It is held out in his palms like an offering (B06).
   - Its halftone dots become stars under the loupe (C08).
   - It appears in Droste, him holding himself holding himself (C10).
   - At the end it lies alone under glass (B46).
   - It is the only image of him the film never alters, except by being looked at.
5. **THE RUNWAY (the drawing that converges).** The same one-point frame returns in every chorus, one stop brighter each
   time:
   - chorus 1: the crowd is people;
   - chorus 2: the crowd is points of light (the noosphere), and he is points while his reflection is still flesh;
   - chorus 3: it is empty and frosted with paperclips;
   - chorus 4: the crowd streams into the point;
   - Ω: the runway itself folds into the point.

   Walking toward us, he is shown; walking away, he goes home.

**Reading the curve.** Cutting speeds up act by act: about 20, then 28, then 50 cuts per minute, bottoming out in 14
cuts of one beat each (125.69-131.99). All 14 share the same fixed point at frame centre, so the eye never moves while the
world changes under it. That is the singularity as editing: every scale converges on the same spot. Two counterpoints
keep it from reading as a pop edit:
- **Verse 2 is one 10.9 s take** under driving drums (B16).
- **The Ilya line is one 5.4 s hold** in the middle of the fastest chorus (B43).

**The release.** After 50 cuts per minute, the loudest 12.7 s of the song get one shot (B45). The speed is inside the
frame (the forward walk, slit-scan, every point streaming) and there is stillness in the cut. The white arrives on bar
84 (151.145) and holds. When the drums stop at 152.963, the white cuts to the smallest possible image: a photograph under
glass. A breath fogs it. The fog evaporates from the edges inward, like every convergence in the film, to one point over
his eye, which goes out exactly on the bass cut (154.781). The photograph holds, clear. The HUD says `01 · BIOSPHERE`
and `CLOCK ×1`: after infinity, one real second. The fade begins at 155.28 (the song's own fade below -40 dBFS), and the
title appears small in the last second. Who breathed on the glass (he, the machine, or the viewer) is left open.
